# Glaze Vault user guide

Glaze Vault keeps your glaze and clay recipes encrypted on your own computer, and works out each
glaze's chemistry as you type, with the same calculator as [Glazecalc](https://glazecalcapp.com).
Nothing is sent anywhere: there is no account, no cloud and no internet connection needed.

> Glaze Vault is in early development. This guide describes what the app does today, and marks
> what is still to come.

## Coming later

- **Printing and export:** a recipe with its analysis, or a batch sheet for a chosen batch size;
  CSV and JSON export; encrypted backups you can restore or open on another computer.
- **Teams:** an administrator, other people with their own passwords, roles such as batch
  operator or formulator, and shared and private collections of recipes. A vault for one person
  can become a team's vault, and go back to one person, at any time.
- **Other computers on your network,** and laptops that take a copy of their part of the vault
  where there is no network, then bring their changes back.
- **Updates** that install themselves.
