# Printing

Open a recipe and choose **Print…** at the top of the window. Choose what to print, then
**Print**. Your computer's print dialog opens, where you can choose a printer, or save a PDF.

Every printout is marked **Confidential**, with when it was printed. If the recipe has changes you
have not saved, the printout says so, and shows the changes.

## A recipe

The recipe's name, its revision and when it was saved, each material with its amount and its
percentage of the whole, your notes, and the unity formula with the SiO₂ : Al₂O₃ ratio, thermal
expansion and loss on ignition.

## A batch sheet

For weighing out a batch. Choose **A batch sheet for weighing out**, type the **batch size**, and
choose grams, kilograms, pounds or ounces.

Each material's weight is worked out so that all of them together make the batch size. Colorants
and other additives count as part of the batch, not on top of it. For example, a recipe of 100
parts base and 10 parts iron oxide, as a 1,100 g batch, needs 1,000 g of base and 100 g of iron
oxide.

The sheet lists, for each material:

- its percentage and its weight;
- a **running total**: what the scale reads after adding it, when everything goes into one bucket;
- a blank **Lot** column for the material's lot number;
- a box to tick when it is weighed.

At the bottom are lines for who weighed the batch, the date, and who checked it.
