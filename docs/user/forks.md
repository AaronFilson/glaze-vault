# When a record was changed in two places

Computers that [sync with a host](joining.md) can each change a record while they cannot reach it:
a laptop in the kiln shed, say, and the studio desktop. When they sync, neither change is thrown
away. The record has two versions, and Glaze Vault says so above it:

> **Shino** was changed in two places from the same revision. You see the version saved …; the
> other was saved …. Choose the one to keep. The others stay in its history.

Every computer shows the same version first: the one that reached the host last.

## Choosing a version

Anyone who may change the record can choose. Look at both first: on a recipe, **Compare…** puts
them side by side. Then choose **Keep this version** or **Keep the other**. The version not kept
stays in the record's history, so you can copy anything from it into the one you kept.

Glaze Vault never mixes the two versions' amounts together: a recipe that is half one person's
and half another's would be neither of the recipes anyone tested.

Until someone chooses, the record works as usual, showing the version listed first. Saving it
saves after that version, and the record stays forked.

## Deleted on another computer

If someone deleted a record on one computer while it was being changed on another, the change is
kept, with a note that it was deleted elsewhere. Choose **Keep it**, or **Delete it** after all.

## Avoiding forks

Before changing a record for a while, [check it out](joining.md#checking-a-record-out): while the
host can be reached, nobody else can save it until you are done.
