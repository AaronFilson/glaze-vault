# Setting up your vault

The first time Glaze Vault opens, it asks you to set up your vault. It takes a minute, and you
need a printer or a pen.

## 1. Choose a password

Type a password twice. It needs at least 8 characters; a few unrelated words, such as
`kiln marmalade bicycle`, are easier to remember and harder to guess than a short word with
symbols. Tick **Show password** to check what you typed.

You type this password each time you open the vault. Glaze Vault does not keep it anywhere: it
uses it to unlock the key that decrypts your recipes.

## 2. Keep your recovery key

Next, Glaze Vault shows 24 words: your **recovery key**. If you forget your password, these words
are the only way back into your vault. Nobody else can recover it for you, including the people
who make Glaze Vault.

- Choose **Print recovery sheet**, or write the words down in order, with their numbers.
- Keep the sheet somewhere safe and away from the computer, such as a locked drawer or a safe.
- Do not keep it as a photo or a file on the same computer.

Then tick **I have printed or written down these words** and choose **Continue**.

## 3. Check the sheet

Glaze Vault asks for three of the words, by number, to be sure your sheet can be read. Type them
from the sheet, not from memory, and choose **Open my vault**. If one is wrong, **Show the words
again** takes you back.

Your vault is ready. See [Recipes and their chemistry](recipes.md).

## Opening and locking

Each time Glaze Vault starts, type your password and choose **Unlock**. Choose **Lock** at the top
of the window to close the vault without quitting: your recipes are hidden until the password is
typed again. Quitting the app locks it too.

> Glaze Vault does not yet lock itself after a while with nobody using it. Lock it, or quit it,
> before you leave the computer.

If you forget your password, see [Using your recovery key](recovery-key.md#using-your-recovery-key).
