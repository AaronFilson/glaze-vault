# Phones and tablets

Glaze Vault on an iPhone, iPad or Android phone works with the same encrypted vaults as on a
computer. At the bottom of the screen are **Recipes**, **Mix log**, **Firing log**, and **More**
(for **Materials**, **Batch sheets** in a shared vault, **Settings**, and **Lock**).

## Joining your studio's vault, or making your own

When Glaze Vault first opens on a phone or tablet, you can:

- **Join a vault on this network** to sync with a shared vault a computer serves. Point the camera
  at the QR code on the admin's screen (or choose the host found on the network and type the
  pairing code): see [Joining from a phone or tablet](joining.md#joining-from-a-phone-or-tablet).
  People, roles, backups of the vault, and the audit log are looked after on a computer; the phone
  keeps your recipes, batch sheets, mix log and firing log with you at the scale and the kilns,
  even when Wi-Fi is out of reach.
- **Set up a vault** on the phone itself, with a password and a 24-word recovery key, or **Restore
  from a backup** (including **Restore from a backup on this phone** after restoring a new phone
  from iCloud or Google): see [Backups on a phone](backups.md#on-a-phone).

A phone or tablet cannot serve a vault to other devices. To share a phone's own vault across a
network later, make a backup on the phone, restore it on a computer, and
[serve it from there](hosting.md).

## Locking

Like on a computer, Glaze Vault locks after 15 minutes without use (see
[Locking by itself](settings.md#locking-by-itself)). On a phone or tablet it also locks shortly
after you switch to another app or lock the screen, keeping any unsaved edits encrypted so they
are waiting when you unlock.

## Firing photos and printing

- In the [firing log](firing-log.md#photos), **Take photo** opens the camera directly so you can
  photograph test tiles and bars as you unload the kiln, and **Choose photo…** picks from your
  photo library.
- Wherever a computer opens a print dialog (**Print…** on a recipe or batch sheet, **Print mix
  record**, blend weights and bar labels, or **Save recovery sheet as PDF…**), a phone or tablet
  makes a PDF and opens the system save/share sheet so you can print it or keep it safe offline.
